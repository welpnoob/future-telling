About

One of the main reasons that someone will decide whether or not to go play basketball is if they have someone to go with or not. This website was created to establish a stronger, more well connected basketball community in Malaysia in the future. It was first started when I noticed a lack of a platform or app to connect basketball players in Malaysia to other locals and foreigners. I saw this as an opportunity to strengthen the basketball community in Malaysia in the future with a platform that allows basketball players of similar skill levels in the similar area to connect and play. My hope for this website is that in the future this will be the platform that is used whenever someone wants to start a pickup game or just shoot around. Another hope for the future is to expand this to other activities such as going to the gym, rock climbing, pickleball, etc. This will remove a barrier for Malaysians participating in physical activity, increasing exercise and overall health for everyone.
